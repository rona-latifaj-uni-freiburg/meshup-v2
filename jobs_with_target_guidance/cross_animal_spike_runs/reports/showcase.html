Failure case spotlight: elephant ↔ giraffe

The hardest pair in the set. The giraffe's long thin neck and the elephant's trunk/head region are structurally mismatched — fixed topology cannot invent new anatomy, Chamfer distance is set-based rather than point-to-point, and thin parts are prone to folding and sliding under optimization. This is a structural limit of the representation, not a failed optimization run.

This pipeline is only as good as the segmentation feeding it — PartField buckets ensure topology fits and points stay in-region, but they are not point-to-point correspondence. Adding true semantic point matching (e.g. a DenseMatcher-style correspondence prior) is the most direct path to closing this gap; see Future Work in the report.
